    Apartments

    Extra charges

    Charge per day for Extra Bed, Firewood and Gas. On a booking, the amount is this rate × the number of days used. A new rate applies only when you set an item to Yes after the change. Past bookings keep their saved amounts.

    Performance targets

    What the Performance tab compares you with. Leave the room rate target at 0 to compare with your own 12-month average.

    OTA commission (%)

    Used to estimate what each channel costs you. 0 means a direct booking.

    Closed dates

    Dates an apartment, or the whole property, is closed, or closed to direct bookings only (OTAs can still sell them). They show on the Availability grid, and the booking form warns before a booking is saved on them.
